Oleh — Ahmad Zidane S, 1 Oktober 2026

After falling into the abyss in the Great Orcus Labyrinth, Hajime lost his left arm to the Claw Bear monster on the first floor. After eating Twin-tailed Wolf meat, which caused his body wither away, He immediately drank the Ambrosia he found in the abyss to counter the poisonous effects of the monster meat, to which his body began to heal and grow stronger and becoming more muscular while growing 10 cm in height. Due to the stress of the revitalization, his hair turned white, his eyes blood-red and his body gained tattoo-like crimson-red veins. Inside of his body, his normal red blood cells turned into a purple spiked blood cells, a sign of his monsterization.

After successfully completing the Great Orcus Labyrinth, Hajime's outfit consisted of a black coat with crimson-red linings, a gray vest, a white dress shirt, a black cravat terminated by a ring tied around his neck, black pants, black armed boots, and a gun holster strapped to each of of his thighs. He also replaced his lost left arm with a metal prosthetic arm, and his lost right eye with an artifact called the Demon Eye that he covered with a black eyepatch. His appearance has been described to look like an anime character, something Hajime is painfully aware of.
